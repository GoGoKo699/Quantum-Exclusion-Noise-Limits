# Quantum Exclusion Noise Limits

**Research stage — PRX Quantum target; no release or publication claim.**

How much preparation corruption can a receiver tolerate while still naming a state that was **not** prepared?

We study three distinct pure qubit states whose Bloch triangle strictly contains the origin. The receiver obtains repeated copies of one unknown state and outputs an excluded label. The models cover unknown-location adversarial replacement and independent mixing, allowing unrestricted collective measurements.

## Candidate results under validation

- **Adversarial threshold:** perfect exclusion for up to (t) corrupted copies is conjectured to hold precisely when (n\ge3t+1), under a precisely specified adversary class. The unrestricted necessity proof remains to be integrated and checked.
- **Independent noise:** the proposed small-noise leading exponent is (rac13\log(1/\delta)+O(1)). Uniform asymptotic bounds and the order of limits remain proof obligations.
